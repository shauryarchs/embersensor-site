Early wildfire risk detection for smarter home protection

EmberSensor is an IoT-connected wildfire monitoring and response system designed to detect dangerous conditions earlier, assess real-time fire risk around a home, and support faster protective action through alerts, live monitoring, and smart sprinkler activation.

Why EmberSensor exists

Wildfires can spread quickly, and ember-driven ignition can threaten homes before people have enough time to react. EmberSensor was created to bring local sensing, weather awareness, wildfire hotspot data, live monitoring, and automated response together into one connected system.

Project phases

EmberSensor has grown in two phases: first detecting danger and alerting, then finding and putting out a fire directly.

Phase 2

Find and suppress

A thermal camera on a motorized slider and pan-and-tilt arm finds a fire, aims a spray nozzle at it, and sprays water until it is out. It was tested on a live fire and can be aimed and sprayed remotely from the iPhone app.

What the system already does

EmberSensor is already more than an idea. A working prototype has been built with several real capabilities that can be demonstrated today.

Real-time local sensing

Tracks temperature, smoke, and flame-related conditions around the home environment.

Weather-aware risk analysis

Uses live weather conditions such as heat, humidity, wind, and rain to better understand risk.

Nearby wildfire awareness

Integrates satellite-based wildfire hotspot data to detect active fire activity near the surrounding area.

Integrated fire risk scoring

Combines local sensors, weather, and nearby fire activity into a single live fire risk level.

Whole-yard sprinkler activation

Starts the home sprinkler zone automatically when the fire risk level is high.

Mobile and live monitoring

Includes live status access, map-based fire awareness, and connected camera visibility.

Thermal fire targeting

A thermal camera on a motorized slider and pan-and-tilt arm finds the hottest spot and aims at it.

Targeted fire suppression

Sprays water directly on the detected fire and stops once the hotspot is gone.

Remote aim and spray

The iPhone app can move the nozzle and start or stop a short manual spray.
